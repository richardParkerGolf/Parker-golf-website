WHAT A LESSON MIGHT LOOK LIKE

Start with what it needs to enable.

Take something as fundamental as your grip. Rather than beginning with a textbook picture, we might start with a question:

What does your grip need to enable you to do?

Change something. Try it. What changed? There is still a purpose, and I’m still coaching. But we’ve started with function, not an ideal picture.

This is the kind of exploration a lesson might involve: a clear question, a purposeful task and enough freedom to discover what changes.
