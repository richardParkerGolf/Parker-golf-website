THE PARKER GOLF APPROACH

Guided
discovery.

You're not left to figure it out alone. And you're not simply given a swing to copy.

I use my experience as a coach to help identify what matters, give you the knowledge you need and create purposeful tasks that make the feedback clear.

The guidance gives the exploration direction. The discovery belongs to you.

Rather than prescribing exactly how your body should move, we'll explore what you need to do to produce the result you're looking for.

RICHARD PARKER

Coaching, not a prescription.

I've played golf for more than 30 years and know what it feels like to chase positions, techniques and the next answer.

Parker Golf grew from a different idea: good coaching can give you direction without taking the learning away from you.

My job is to guide the process, make the feedback useful and help you discover a game you understand and can trust.

IS PARKER GOLF FOR YOU?

If you want to be involved in your own learning, probably.

Parker Golf is for golfers who are curious. You don't need to know anything about swing theory, and you don't need to arrive with a technical question. You just need to be willing to explore.
