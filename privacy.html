PRIVACY

Privacy policy.

Parker Golf respects your privacy and only uses personal information where it is needed to respond to you, provide coaching services or manage a booking.

Information you provide

If you contact Parker Golf, I may receive information such as your name, email address and anything you choose to include in your enquiry. Information you provide when making a booking is handled through the SumUp Bookings service.

How your information is used

Your information may be used to respond to enquiries, arrange and provide coaching, manage communication about your sessions, and keep reasonable business and financial records.

Booking through SumUp

The booking link on this website takes you to SumUp Bookings, a third-party service. Information you submit there is also subject to SumUp's own privacy practices and terms.

Sharing your information

Parker Golf does not sell your personal information. Information may be processed by service providers where this is necessary to operate the business, manage bookings or meet legal obligations.

How long information is kept

Personal information is kept only for as long as it is reasonably needed for the purpose it was collected, ongoing coaching relationships, legitimate business administration, or applicable legal and accounting requirements.

Cookies and analytics

This Parker Golf website does not currently use its own advertising cookies or analytics tracking. Third-party services you choose to visit, such as SumUp Bookings, may use cookies under their own policies.

Your rights

UK data protection law gives you rights over your personal information. Depending on the circumstances, these can include asking for access to your information, correction, deletion, restriction of its use, or objecting to certain processing.

Contact

For a privacy question or request, email hello@parkergolf.uk.

This policy may be updated if the way Parker Golf handles personal information changes.
